## What the setup did not catch

- The health signal watched representation diversity (PR of layers[12]),
  which stayed above its floor while the behaviour went to two actions.
- With the dose at most 0.011 units and lambda near 0, A1 as run is in
  effect the queued `A1-lam none` and `A1 none` arms.

## Changes made for the rest of the queue

- `--health pr+act` (be64338): a second dual on the entropy of the action
  tokens over the restricted vocabulary, floor 0.8 x its early value (about
  0.35 nats; early entropy 0.40–0.46). It adds
  lambda_act x relu(floor − H) / floor to the loss and the dose becomes
  max(lambda, lambda_act). Smoke-tested on replayed data: runs, lambda_act
  responds to dips below the floor.
- The queue after A1-act (already running, PR-only health as registered):
  A1, A1+self, A1+bored, A2, A3 with `pr+act`, then `A1 fixed:1` with PR-only
  health (energy on from the start, no action dual, to see whether energy
  alone holds the behaviour). `A1-lam none` and `A1 none` were dropped as
  duplicates of A1 as run.
